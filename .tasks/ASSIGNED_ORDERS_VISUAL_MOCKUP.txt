                                INTERACTIVE ELEMENTS

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BUTTONS:

  [Accept]              - Accept the order (appears on yellow "assigned" orders)
  [Start Delivery]      - Mark as on delivery (appears on blue/purple orders)
  
  Hover effect: Button color deepens when you hover over it
  Loading state: Button shows "Accepting..." or "Starting..." while processing
  Disabled state: Button becomes translucent if already processing

PAGE SELECTOR:

  [10 per page ▼]  - Click to open dropdown menu
    └─ 5 per page
    └─ 10 per page (currently selected)
    └─ 20 per page
    └─ 50 per page
  
  Selected option is highlighted
  Page automatically resets to 1 when changing

PAGINATION BUTTONS:

  [← Previous]  - Click to go to previous page
                - Disabled (grayed out) on page 1
                - Enabled on any other page
  
  [1] [2] [3]   - Page number buttons
                - Click any number to jump to that page
                - Current page is highlighted blue
                - Other pages have gray background
  
  [Next →]      - Click to go to next page
                - Disabled (grayed out) on last page
                - Enabled on any other page

                                  TYPICAL WORKFLOW

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. OPEN PAGE
   ↓
   Sees table with 10 orders (default)
   Current page: 1

2. VIEW ORDERS
   ↓
   Scans table for action items
   Notices yellow orders (need accepting)
   Notices blue orders (ready to deliver)

3. ACCEPT ORDER
   ↓
   Clicks [Accept] on yellow order
   Button shows "Accepting..."
   Page refreshes
   Order moves to blue (accepted)

4. START DELIVERY
   ↓
   Clicks [Start Delivery] on blue order
   Button shows "Starting..."
   Page refreshes
   Order moves to green (on delivery)

5. BROWSE MORE ORDERS
   ↓
   Clicks [Next →] to see more orders
   Table shows orders 11-20
   Page indicator shows "Page 2 of 5"

6. SWITCH TO LARGER VIEW
   ↓
   Clicks [50 per page ▼]
   Selects "50 per page"
   Page resets to 1
   All 47 orders visible (or up to 50)
   Page indicator shows "Page 1 of 1"
